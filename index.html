The narration is AI-generated, prepared in advance, and included with the app. No voice service is contacted while you listen.

Drift saves its files, both voices, and background sounds for offline use, about 29 MB in total. The player has no accounts, analytics, or session history. Your hosting provider can see ordinary page and file requests.

Your session length, voice, pauses, fade, volume, background sound, and sound volume stay in this browser. You can reset those preferences below.

Soft rain uses a CC0 recording by myLoop. Pink noise and Brown noise are generated locally. All three sounds are included in the app.
